What Mimi Studios stands for

The essence guides every decision — from which client to take on to which typeface goes on a thumbnail.

Positioning

A motion design practice for founders and brands who need their product or story to move — literally — with the same craft as a big studio, at independent speed.

Who it's for

SaaS founders, app teams, and brands who need explainer motion, product demos, and social-native animation that doesn't look templated.

Personality

Precise, quietly confident, technically fluent. Talks like a craftsperson, not a salesperson.

Not this

Not a stock-template shop. Not loud for the sake of it. Not vague about process or pricing.

Section — Voice00:00:05:00

How Mimi Studios talks

Four principles that apply to captions, proposals, and DMs alike.

Be specific
SAY"Cut load-perceived time with a 400ms skeleton-to-content transition."
NOT"Made the app feel faster."
Own the craft language
SAY"Custom ease curve, no default easing."
NOT"Smooth animations!"
Confident, not salesy
SAY"Here's the direction — happy to walk through the reasoning."
NOT"I think you'll LOVE this!! 🔥🔥"
Short sentences, real nouns
SAY"Delivery: Friday. Two rounds of revisions included."
NOT"We aim to deliver in a timely manner pending feedback."
