The mark

A bottom-anchored M — two strokes rising to peaks, dipping to a valley in the middle. Three approved colorways, each layered in two values for depth.

Primary — signal only
Featured — signal + ease
Reversed — light bg
clear space = 1× mark height, all sides
Correct

One of the three approved colorways, full clear space respected, never smaller than 20px.

Avoid

No recoloring outside the three approved colorways — including gradients or hue shifts.

Avoid

Don't stretch or distort the proportions — scale uniformly only.

Section — Voice00:00:05:00

How it talks

Four principles — captions, proposals, and DMs alike.

Be specific
SAY"Cut load-perceived time with a 400ms skeleton-to-content transition."
NOT"Made the app feel faster."
Own the craft language
SAY"Custom ease curve, no default easing."
NOT"Smooth animations!"
Confident, not salesy
SAY"Here's the direction — happy to walk through the reasoning."
NOT"I think you'll LOVE this!! 🔥🔥"
Short sentences, real nouns
SAY"Delivery: Friday. Two rounds of revisions included."
NOT"We aim to deliver in a timely manner pending feedback."
